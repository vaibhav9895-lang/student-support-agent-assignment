# Sample Student Handbook
This is fictional demonstration content created for an academic assignment. It is not an official institutional policy.

## Fee Payment and Deadlines
Students should pay fees by the deadline published by the institution's Accounts Office. Late fees may apply after the published deadline. The exact amount, deadline and any exception must be confirmed with the Accounts Office.

## Payment Portal Failure
If the payment portal is unavailable near a deadline, students should save evidence of the failed transaction, including the date and time, and contact the Accounts Office. A portal failure does not automatically guarantee an extension to the next working day. The Accounts Office confirms the applicable next steps.

## Course Change Requests
Course-change requests should be submitted through the academic office using the institution's prescribed process. Requests are subject to eligibility, seat availability, academic rules and the published deadline. Students should contact the academic office for a decision on an individual case.

## Scholarship Eligibility and Status
Scholarship eligibility depends on the criteria of the applicable scholarship scheme and the required supporting documents. Application status should be confirmed through the scholarship office or the official student portal. A status marked “Under Review” is not an approval.

## Refund Requests
Refund requests should be submitted to the Accounts Office with the relevant payment evidence and student details. Eligibility, deductions and processing timelines depend on the applicable institutional rules. The sample handbook does not specify a guaranteed refund amount or processing period.

## Student Support Requests
Students may raise a support request for issues involving the student portal, fee records or academic administration. The request should include a short description of the issue and avoid including passwords, OTPs or other credentials.
